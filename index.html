FAQ

Questions, answered.

What can I ask about?

Math, physics and programming, from algebra, probability and calculus to forces, energy, waves and circuits, and on to algorithms, data structures and how code runs. Unsafe or non-educational requests are declined, and the credit comes back (up to three times a day).

How long does a custom lesson take?

About a minute to build. A lesson takes most people 10–15 minutes to work through.

How is this different from asking a chatbot?

A chatbot gives you text. Axiom gives you a lesson you do: simulations, draggable graphs, code you step through, predictions you commit to before seeing the answer, and mastery checks at the end.

How accurate is it?

Every generated lesson passes automated checks before it's shown: quiz answers are re-checked, arithmetic in worked examples is evaluated, and physics simulations run on real equations. The 60+ Originals are prepared in advance and pass the same checks.

Does it work offline?

The Originals work fully offline. Building a new custom lesson needs a connection.

Is there tracking or ads?

No ads and no cross-app tracking. Custom lessons are written by a third-party AI model (xAI), called from our servers. See the privacy policy.

Which devices?

iPhone, on the App Store.
